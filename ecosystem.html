Ecosystem

An Ecosystem Designed for Growth

AgricFi combines DeFi, tokenization, and real-world farm verification to create a market for agriculture-backed digital assets.

💰

DeFi Funding Hub

The financial backbone of AgricFi. Bridges the funding gap between smallholder farmers and global investors through an open, decentralized gateway for agricultural financing.

  • On-chain Verification & Escrow
  • Tokenized Farm Assets
  • Dynamic APYs based on farm performance
🌿

Sustainability Ledger

AgricFi's proof-of-impact layer. Captures and records all farm activities that contribute to environmental sustainability with full on-chain transparency for investors and regulators.

  • On-chain Impact Verification
  • Carbon Credit Recording
  • Investor & Regulator Transparency
🪙

$AGFI Token Utility

The $AGFI token powers all activities within the ecosystem — the transactional, governance, and incentive engine. Stake $AFT to earn yield and participate in decision making.

  • Staking for Yield & Governance
  • Marketplace Fee Settlement
  • Fixed Supply with Controlled Emissions
📊

Yield Dashboard

The investor's control center — a real-time performance monitor aggregating on-chain data, IoT metrics, and market analytics into one intuitive interface.

  • Real-time IoT & On-chain Data
  • Portfolio Performance Tracking
  • Market Analytics & Projections
🖼️

Farm NFTs

Transform real farmland and crop batches into verified digital assets. Each NFT represents a fraction of a real-world farm, making agricultural ownership accessible to anyone globally.

  • Verified Asset Minting with Geolocation
  • Fractional Co-ownership Model
  • Dynamic IoT & Satellite Data Integration
🏪

Marketplace

AgricFi's digital trading hub — buy, sell, and auction farm NFTs, produce tokens, and sustainability credits within a secure, decentralized on-chain marketplace.

  • NFT & Produce Token Trading
  • MetaMask & Trust Wallet Support
  • Real-time Market Analytics
